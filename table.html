How to read the table

Dealer zone

The dealer shows one card face-up and keeps one face-down. Right now the dealer is showing a King, worth 10.

Player zone

Your cards are both face-up. A 10 and a 6 add up to 16, which is a tricky hand to decide on.

The deck

New cards come from the deck when you hit. The next card here would give you 19.
